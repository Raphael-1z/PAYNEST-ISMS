# PayNest ISMS Scope

*PayNest is a fictional company created for a portfolio project.*

Version: 1.0 | Owner: Information Security Officer | Date: 25 November 2026

## Scope statement

The ISMS of PayNest applies to the protection of information used to provide credit, savings, deposit-taking and wallet services through its Tamale office, mobile app, website, USSD and field agents.

## Included

- **People:** all 25 staff (office and remote, in Ghana) and field agents
- **Information:** customer personal data, accounts, wallets, transaction, credit and staff records
- **Systems:** in-house software, core systems, databases, Microsoft 365 (non-sensitive use only), office and mobile devices
- **Connectivity and power:** MTN internet, Starlink backup, UPS and backup generator

## Hosting and backup

- **Primary:** PayNest servers at Onix Data Centre, Accra
- **Backup:** encrypted backup server at the Tamale office

## Suppliers and dependencies

Onix, Microsoft, MTN, Starlink, USSD provider, independent security tester

## Exclusions

- **Customers' own phones and computers:** PayNest does not control them. The security of PayNest's app, website, USSD service and customer data remains in scope.
- **Suppliers' own equipment and buildings:** the suppliers manage these. PayNest's management of supplier risks remains in scope.
